# ⚡ A Birthday Tribute to Subhickshun Naren
### *"From Classmates to Brothers • 2014 → 2026 → FOREVER"*

A cinematic, emotional, athletic **One-Page Birthday Tribute Website** built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Custom CSS**. 

> *"Some relationships are created by blood. Ours was created by time, memories, random moments, fights, laughs, and a thousand little things that somehow became a lifetime."*

---

## 🎬 Cinematic Narrative Flow

1. **Athletic Starter Intro** (`READY?` ➔ `SET.` ➔ `GO.` countdown).
2. **Hero Tribute** (`/assets/subhickshun-hero.jpg`, *"FOR THE ATHLETE I'LL ALWAYS REMEMBER."*).
3. **01 / A Memory** (*"Maybe this is just a website to everyone else. But to me, it's a memory."*).
4. **More Than A Friend** (*"YOU ARE MORE THAN A FRIEND. YOU ARE MY NON-BLOOD BROTHER."*).
5. **From Classmates to Brothers (2014 → 2026 → FOREVER)**:
   - `2014` — 4th Standard Classmates
   - `Neighbour Friend` — Beyond the classroom
   - `11th Classmate` — Reconnecting as someone important
   - `Bus Friend` — Ordinary rides turned lifelong memories (with animated road speed lines)
   - `12th Grade` — Best Friend
   - `Now` — My Brother
   - Typography Evolution: `FRIEND ➔ BEST FRIEND ➔ BROTHER`
6. **2014 — 2026** (*12 YEARS. COUNTLESS MEMORIES. ONE BROTHER. 2014 ➔ 2026 ➔ ∞ AND FOREVER.*).
7. **Life Will Change** (*"Life will change. You will change... But no matter what you become... I'LL ALWAYS REMEMBER YOU. Not just as the athlete... But as my brother."*).
8. **The Athlete Memory** (*"I WILL ALWAYS REMEMBER THIS VERSION OF YOU. The athlete. The dedication. The discipline."*).
9. **The Moments I'd Keep** (Masonry photo memory wall with light leaks & full-screen lightbox preview).
10. **Motion Never Stopped** (Kinetic telemetry symbolizing the unstoppable 12-year bond).
11. **Featured Photo** (`/assets/subhickshun-featured.jpg` with *"KEEP RUNNING."*).
12. **The Letter From Your Friend** (Full updated letter ending with `"YOUR FRIEND, FOREVER."`).
13. **Final Black Screen** (`2014` ➔ `2014 — 2026` ➔ `AND STILL... FOREVER.` ➔ `MORE THAN A FRIEND. MORE THAN A MEMORY. MY BROTHER.`).
14. **Final Birthday Dedication** (*"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, SUBHICKSHUN. To the athlete who inspired me. To the friend who stayed. To the brother I found without sharing blood."*).
15. **Final CTA & Footer** (*"THE JOURNEY ISN'T OVER. Keep going →"*).

## 🖼️ How to Add or Replace Photos

Simply drop your photos into `public/assets/` using these filenames (or adjust paths in [`src/data/tributeData.js`](file:///e:/subi/src/data/tributeData.js)):

| Filename | Location / Section |
|---|---|
| `public/assets/subhickshun-hero.jpg` | Hero background |
| `public/assets/athlete-action-01.jpg` | "The Athlete Memory" |
| `public/assets/subhickshun-featured.jpg` | "Keep Running" Full-width Photo |
| `public/assets/final-memory.jpg` | Final Photo Memory |
| `public/assets/memory-01.jpg` ... `memory-10.jpg` | "The Moments I'd Keep" Photo Memory Wall |
